My 5 daily habits

I didn't do these all at once. I added one every couple of weeks until it felt normal.

  1. Protein first, every meal

    Eggs in the morning, chicken or fish at lunch and dinner. I eat the protein before anything else on the plate. It keeps me full way longer.“Your doctor said 'eat less'? Mm-mm. Eat smarter.”

  2. Rootation gummies with breakfast

    Every morning with my coffee (I follow the label on the jar). They're part of my routine to support healthy blood sugar, and they've helped with my cravings and that afternoon crash. And no pills to swallow, which I love.

  3. A 10-minute walk after meals

    Just around the block. Even in a hundred-and-five-degree Texas summer, I'd go early in the morning or after dinner. Those little walks added up more than anything.

  4. Strength training 3x a week

    Light dumbbells in my living room, 20 to 30 minutes. Squats, lunges, rows, and presses. Building muscle is what changed the shape of my body.

  5. Cut the sugar (slowly)

    I didn't go cold turkey. I swapped one sugary thing at a time. Sweet tea was the hardest one for this Texas girl!
