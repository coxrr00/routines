Flourless potato buns

No flour, no yeast. Makes a small batch.

1potato, boiled and mashed smooth
2eggs
1/2 cupthick Greek yogurt (thin yogurt makes them too soft)
1 tspbaking powder, plus a pinch of salt
1egg yolk to brush on top, and sesame seeds

Mix it all smooth, spoon into mounds on a lined tray, brush with yolk, sprinkle sesame. Bake at 350F for about 20 to 25 minutes until golden.

Blueberry lime drink

Squeeze the lime on the crushed berries and watch them turn red.

1 cupblueberries
1 tspcinnamon
1/2lime, juiced
1 glasswater

Blend it all. The lime keeps the color bright instead of going dull, and a cup of blueberries gives you about three and a half grams of fiber.

Banana French toast, no bread

Cook it like an omelet and fold it.

1ripe banana, mashed
2eggs
pinchcinnamon and a little vanilla

Whisk it smooth, pour into a buttered pan on low to medium heat, let it set, then fold it in half. It comes out soft and custardy. Top with blueberries.
