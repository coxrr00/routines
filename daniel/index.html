My 6 daily habits

I didn't do these all at once. I added one every couple of weeks until it felt normal.

  1. Soup before rice

    A small bowl of soup first, the way my grandmother served every meal. Then the rest of the plate. It slows me down, and I stopped going back for seconds.“Eat like Grandma, not like the drive-thru.”

  2. Something fermented with every meal

    A little kimchi on the side, doenjang in my soup, or a bowl of plain yogurt. Grandma never put a meal on the table without it, so neither do I.

  3. Barley tea instead of soda

    I keep a jug of cold roasted barley tea in the fridge. No sugar. After 35 years of energy drinks at a desk job, this was the big one.

  4. Rootation gummies with breakfast

    Every morning with breakfast (I follow the label on the jar). They're part of my routine to support healthy blood sugar, and they've helped with my cravings and that afternoon slump. And no pills to swallow.

  5. A 15-minute walk after dinner

    Out the door as soon as the dishes are done. Rain or shine. Those little walks added up more than anything.

  6. Strength class twice a week

    I started light and felt silly. Now it's the best part of my week. Building muscle is what changed the shape of my body.
